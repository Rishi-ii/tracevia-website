OVERVIEW

Insurance fraud poses significant financial and operational risks. At Tracevia, our Motor and Property Claim Investigation services help insurers, TPAs, corporates, and legal professionals verify the authenticity of claims through scientific investigation, field verification, and forensic analysis. We deliver evidence-based reports that support informed decision-making and stand up to legal scrutiny.
